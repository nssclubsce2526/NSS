About the scheme

Service that shapes citizens

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a community-service programme under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. Our club encourages students to engage in volunteer activities focused on community development and nation-building. Alongside social impact, it nurtures soft skills — communication, collaboration and organisation — enriching each volunteer's personality and academic journey.
